    Build a ship
    1. Pick a hull on the left — the join window's tier decides which are allowed.
    2. Click a mount on the ship, then a weapon from the list. Hullmods, vents and capacitors live on the tabs.
    3. Save — the game checks every edit; a valid ship appears in the join window's variant picker.

    Shortcuts

    Editor

    F2the codex — over a hull, weapon, hullmod or wing row: that entry
    Escclose the wing picker, else the weapon picker, else a hover panel
    Ctrl-Z / Ctrl-Yundo / redo (hold: repeat)
    Ctrl-Ssave
    click a mountits weapon list · shift+click: the last weapon again · right-click: unarm
    click a weapon rowarm the selected mount · right-click a loadout or Groups row: unarm
    right-click a weapon rowfill the empty mounts of this size / every compatible mount · put it everywhere · remove it everywhere · favourite (pinned to the top) · codex — while the menu is open, 1–9 and 0 pick its rows
    shift+right-click a weapon rowtake that weapon off the selected mount
    Tab / Shift+Tabwith a mount selected: the next / previous empty mount (large → small, by type, bow → stern; any mount once all are armed) · on Groups: the next row's picker
    Q / Ethe previous / next panel tab
    W A S Dthe nearest mount in that direction (the hull list keeps W / S while it has the focus)
    SpaceDone in a weapon or wing picker · else open the wheel's row (the loadout, Groups or the bay list)
    click off a mounton the ship, or on the panel's empty space: Done (the mount or bay is deselected)
    mouse 4 / 5back / forward through the hulls, tabs and pickers you visited (in the codex: its own back / forward)
    ↑ ↓ or W Sin the hull list: the previous / next hull

    Editor, continued

    click a hullmodadd → build in (S-mod) → remove · right-click: remove · shift+right-click: build in / un-build directly
    Groups: a numbermove the weapon there · shift: every same weapon · click a group row: Linked ⇄ Alternating
    Vents / Capacitorsdrag the slider · ± buttons (hold: repeat · shift: ten) · mouse wheel anywhere on the strip: the nearest row, one per tick (shift: ten)
    Ordnance points barone segment per item, biggest first, weapons in their mount colours · hover: what it is · click: its mount, hullmod or bay · right-click: take it off the ship · shift+click a hullmod: build it in
    wheel, a mount or bay selectedcycle that mount's weapon / that bay's wing — from anywhere on the page (the next / previous one that fits)
    wheel over a mount or baynone selected: over a mount on the ship or its loadout row — select it and cycle · over a bay chip or row — cycle that bay's wing in place
    wheel, nothing selectedmoves the row cursor of the open tab (the loadout, Groups, the bay list) from almost anywhere · Space: open its picker · the hull list, the vents / capacitors strip and the cards keep their own scroll
    shift + wheel over the shipzoom the ship in / out (resets when you pick another hull)
    drag the shipzoomed in past ×1.00: move it around the stage (it re-centres at ×1.00 or on another hull)
    Settings (header)how the ship is drawn (whole pixel steps, crisp blocks, Lanczos …) — remembered on this machine
    Skills (stats column)all captain skills and commander bonuses (deprecated ones excluded) applied to the numbers and ammo — the OP budget never moves
